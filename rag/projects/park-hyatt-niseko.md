# Park Hyatt Niseko

**Location:** 328-47 Aza Iwaobetsu, Kutchan, Abuta District, Hokkaido, Japan

**Tags:** hotel, living

## Overview

Nestled at the base of Mount Annupuri, the Park Hyatt Niseko Hanazono celebrates natural beauty through quiet luxury and refined craftsmanship. The interior design draws inspiration from the surrounding landscape—snow, wood, and stone.

## Details

Within this context, TCC Carpets designed a series of bespoke floor coverings that reflect the rhythm of nature and the Japanese aesthetic of restraint, balancing visual calm with tactile warmth.

## Notes

Subtle gradations and organic patterns complement wood and stone interiors across suites and public areas.
