# United Nations NYC

**Location:** United Nations Plaza, New York, NY

## Overview

The United Nations Headquarters in New York stands as a symbol of international diplomacy and cultural exchange.

## Details

For the Oman Heritage Walk, TCC Carpets designed bespoke floor coverings that honor Omani heritage and craftsmanship, creating a welcoming environment that bridges tradition with the UN's mission of global unity.

## Notes

Traditional Omani motifs and earthy tones reflect the cultural narrative of the heritage installation.
